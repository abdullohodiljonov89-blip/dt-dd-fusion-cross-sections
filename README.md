# Energy Dependence and Comparative Analysis of D–T and D–D Fusion Cross Sections

## Overview

This project investigates the energy dependence of deuterium–tritium (D–T) and deuterium–deuterium (D–D) fusion cross sections using published experimental measurements and theoretical parameterizations.

The study compares the D–T cross section with the combined cross section of the two principal D–D fusion branches over a selected range of center-of-mass energies.

## Research objectives

- Examine the energy dependence of D–T and D–D fusion cross sections.
- Convert laboratory-frame energies to the center-of-mass frame where required.
- Compare published experimental data with the Bosch–Hale parameterization.
- Evaluate the sensitivity of the comparison to data selection and uncertainty assumptions.

## Data and references

The analysis draws on published experimental measurements and theoretical work, including:

- Arnold et al. (1952), Los Alamos report LA-1479.
- Jarmie, Brown, and Hardekopf (1984), *Physical Review C*.
- Brown and Jarmie (1990), *Physical Review C*.
- Bosch and Hale (1992), *Nuclear Fusion*, and the 1993 erratum.
- Tumino et al. (2014), *The Astrophysical Journal*.

Full bibliographic details, data provenance, and any applicable reuse conditions will be documented as the repository develops.

## Repository status

This repository is being prepared to support transparent and reproducible analysis. The data files, calculation scripts, model coefficients, and validation procedures will be added after their contents and scientific consistency have been checked.

**The results should be treated as under verification until the complete analysis can be independently reproduced.**

## Reproducibility

The intended workflow is to document the source data, energy-frame conversions, theoretical model implementation, uncertainty treatment, and figure-generation procedures so that readers can inspect and reproduce the analysis.
